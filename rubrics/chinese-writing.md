# 中國語文 卷二 寫作能力 — scoring rubric (2024+ format)

Status: **draft v0.1 (2026-10-07), awaiting review by a Chinese teacher.**

How this rubric is sourced:
- **Official** parts are from public HKEAA documents, quoted with a `[key]` that refers to the sources table at the bottom.
- **Distilled** parts are concrete markers drawn from the HKEAA level exemplars (2024, 2025; #1–#3 of each level). Each one is cited like `2025 L4#2`. Exemplar #4 of every level is held out as test data and was not used.
- **Estimate** parts are our own conventions, used where HKEAA publishes nothing.

Scope: 乙部 命題寫作 is scored now. 甲部 實用寫作 is described for reference only and isn't scored until a later phase.

---

## 1. Paper structure (official)

| | 甲部 實用寫作 | 乙部 命題寫作 |
|---|---|---|
| Share of Paper 2 / subject | 30% / 13.5% | 70% / 31.5% |
| Suggested time | ~45 min | ~1 hr 30 min |
| Task | One writing task from 2–3 pieces of material, in 書信／評論／建議書／演講辭／報告／專題文章 | One long essay (or 2–3 short pieces), chosen from the questions set |
| Length | Limit of 550字, punctuation included | Set by each question (see the length caps in §2.3) |
| Marking | One marker (單評) | Two markers (雙評) |

Sources: [AF26] p2, [SEM25] slides 3, 23–26.

## 2. 乙部 scoring (official)

### 2.1 Criteria and maximum marks

| Criterion | Max | What is assessed (verbatim, [SEM25] slides 42–43) |
|---|---|---|
| 內容 | 40 | 立意、內容是否合乎寫作要求；豐富、深刻。取材是否恰當；能彰顯立意。闡述是否合理、飽滿。 |
| 表達 | 30 | 用詞是否精確、豐富（主）。文句是否簡潔流暢（主）。表達手法是否純熟靈活（輔）。 |
| 結構 | 20 | 段落區分是否明晰。起承轉合是否自然。輕重詳略是否恰當。 |
| 標點字體 | 10 | 字體為主，標點為輔。標點使用在於準確和靈活，不在於數量多少。 |
| 錯別字 | 3 | 0–1個：3分；2–4個：2分；5–7個：1分；8個或以上：0分。**重錯不計**（a repeated error counts once）。 |
| **Total** | **103** | 按各評分項目，就文章整體評分。 |

### 2.2 品第 scale (the same for 內容, 表達, 結構 and 標點字體) [SEM25] slide 41

| 品 | 品第 → points out of 10 |
|---|---|
| 上品 | 上上 10 · 上中 9 · 上下 8 |
| 中品 | 中上 7 · 中中(上) 6 · 中中(下) 5 · 中下 4 |
| 下品 | 下上 3 · 下中 2 · 下下 1 |
| — | 極差劣／空白 0 |

**Estimate:** the 10-point grade is multiplied by **×4 / ×3 / ×2 / ×1** for 內容 / 表達 / 結構 / 標點字體 to reach 40 / 30 / 20 / 10. HKEAA states the multiplier explicitly only for 甲部 內容 (「倍大為30分」); the ones here are worked out from the maximum marks.

### 2.3 Caps (official, [SEM25] slide 44)
- **Off-topic (離題):** 內容 ≤ 下上 · 表達 and 結構 ≤ 中上 · 標點字體 can still be 上上.
- **Short essays** (character count includes punctuation), cap on 內容: 550–649 → 上上 · 450–549 → 中上 · 300–449 → 中中(下) · under 300 → 下上.
  - This is as printed. The slide heading says 「不足650字」, yet 550–649 is still allowed 上上, so in practice the first real cap starts below 550. Possibly a slide error; see open issues.
- Memorised "model essays" (背誦), where detected, are not marked for that portion [HB25].

### 2.4 錯別字 and 繁體／簡體 (official, [SEM25] slide 45, [QA22] Q2, [QA25] Q1)
- 「繁體字或簡化字均可接受。簡化字以中國政府2013年所頒布之《通用規範漢字表》為據。」
- HKEAA says traditional and simplified may be mixed, even within the same sentence (「同一句句字同時採用繁體字和簡體字」).

| 計作錯別字 (counted as wrong) | 不計作錯別字 (not counted) |
|---|---|
| 繁簡同體 (one character blending traditional and simplified parts) | 一個詞語中，一個簡體字，一個繁體字 |
| 缺部件或增部件 (a missing or extra component) | 字體潦草 (scrawled handwriting, which is judged under 標點字體 instead) |
| 「空格」造成詞意不連貫 (a gap that breaks the word) | 重複錯同一偏旁 (the same wrong radical repeated counts once) |

**Teacher rule, 「繁簡混用」 (decided, Q23: feedback only).**
- The class rule is to write in one script. Every character in the minority script is flagged with its form in the dominant script.
- This is listed **separately from 錯別字** and **does not change the DSE estimate**, which follows HKEAA as above.
- Evidence that HKEAA doesn't penalise mixing: Level 5 scripts include all-simplified scripts (2024 L5#3, 2025 L5#1) and mixed-script scripts (2024 L5#1, 2025 L5#3).

## 3. Level descriptors (official, verbatim, [LD], marked 暫定稿 / provisional)

| Level | 典型表現 |
|---|---|
| 5 | 緊扣主題取材；全面回應語境要求整合資料，並充分發揮。中心突出，內容豐富；用詞妥帖，文句流暢，有文采；條理分明，詳略得當，結構嚴謹。 |
| 4 | 取材切合題意；回應大部分語境要求整合資料，並加以發揮。中心明確，內容具體；用詞準確，文句通順；條理清楚，銜接自然，詳略合宜，結構完整。 |
| 3 | 取材切合題意；回應部分語境要求整合資料，並略加發揮。中心大致明確，意念完整；用詞正確，文句大致通順；條理清楚，詳略大致合宜，結構完整。 |
| 2 | 取材大致切合題意；回應小部分語境要求整合資料，並稍有發揮。能表達完整意念；詞句達意；尚具篇章結構，詳略尚可。 |
| 1 | 稍具扣題意識；粗略回應小部分語境要求。能表達零碎意念；詞句尚可達意；粗具篇章脈絡。 |

The descriptors cover both 甲部 and 乙部. The phrases about 「整合資料」 (integrating the given material) mainly apply to 甲部.

## 4. Level markers for 乙部 (distilled from the exemplars)

Each marker is something the scorer can observe in the text. **The most important finding:** across both years, the main thing that separates the levels is **the depth of the idea (立意) and whether the examples are analysed**. Neatness, script and length matter much less.

### Level 5 (vs Level 4)
- **The idea goes deeper than the question.** An event becomes a principle, often through a symbol or pun tied to the title.
  - The soil is also the dirty business world, and the fake diamond becomes 本心 (2025 L5#1).
  - 「遺忘不等於放下」 → making peace with the past (2024 L5#1).
- **Argument essays concede a point or give counter-examples, and analyse them.**
  - 「無愧」 is limited by 合義, with counter-examples (2024 L5#2).
  - When giving up a promise is responsible, and when keeping one too long is not (2024 L5#3).
  - The argument widens from the individual to society (2025 L5#3).
- **Many examples from different eras and places, each one explained, not just named** (2024 L5#2, L5#3; 2025 L5#3).
- **The stimulus material is reworked, not copied.** It's compressed into an insight, for example a closing 排比 (2025 L5#2).
- **The turning point is set up earlier (伏筆) and paid off later**, not explained in a final paragraph (2025 L5#1, L5#2).
- **Rhetoric feels natural.**
  - 排比 「失去了童年，失去了伙伴，失去了芭比」 (2024 L5#1).
  - A self-implicating question (2024 L5#3).
  - The opening and ending echo through imagery, not by repeating a sentence (2025 L5#2; 2024 L5#1 「風又起，風又止」).
- **The argument builds step by step**, for example meaning → benefit → limit → method (2024 L5#2).
- Length is about 1,150–1,600字. **Messy handwriting and simplified or mixed scripts don't stop a script reaching Level 5.**

### Level 4 (vs Level 5 / Level 3)
- **On topic with a clear device, but the parts sit side by side instead of building.**
  - Photos used as a thread (2024 L4#1).
  - Four parallel scenes, each ending 「問心無愧」 (2024 L4#2).
  - Three episodes (2025 L4#3).
  - 首先／其次／另外 points (2024 L4#3).
- **Reasoning is tacked on at the end:** general comments, a famous quotation, a direct appeal to the reader (2025 L4#2, L4#3).
- **The ideas are correct but common**, for example accept yourself or change your viewpoint, without Level 5's symbolic depth (2025 L4#1).
- **Real feeling, carried by concrete detail.** Grandpa sewing the doll (2024 L4#1). Vivid description and similes, some of them clichés (2025 L4#1, L4#2).
- **The argument holds together and the examples fit,** including a counter-example (2024 L4#3).
- The structure is complete and the opening and ending echo, but endings are often short or rushed (2024 L4#1, L4#3).
- About 950–1,500字. Very neat scripts can still be Level 4, because depth is what holds them back (2024 L4#2).

### Level 3 (vs Level 4 / Level 2)
- **On topic but generic.** Examples are hypothetical or common knowledge, with no first-hand detail (2025 L3#2, L3#3). Examples are only loosely relevant (2024 L3#3).
- **A mechanical framework.** Signposts like 從…說起／接着／然後／最後／總結來說. Examples pile up without a main point (2025 L3#2, L3#3).
- **The position wobbles or sits on the fence.** It contradicts itself (2024 L3#2) or settles for 「兩者並無對錯」 (2024 L3#3). Some facts are wrong (2024 L3#2).
- **Feeling comes through stock phrases:** 十分難過、悲痛欲絕、後悔莫及 (2024 L3#1). On lyrical topics, images pile up and the main idea blurs (2025 L3#1).
- **The ending echoes the opening mechanically:** the first and last sentences are identical (2024 L3#1).
- **Occasional strong lines but little depth.** Sentences are mostly fluent, with few figures of speech and some repeated phrases. There are more corrections than at Level 4.
- About 950–1,250字, which overlaps Level 4, so length can't separate Level 3 from Level 4.

### Level 2 (vs Level 3 / Level 1)
- **Weak grip on the question.** The topic is read only on the surface (treasure = old belongings, 2025 L2#1), or the feeling the question asks for is thin (2024 L2#1).
- **The argument rests on one point and one example.**
  - Examples are only named, never analysed (2025 L2#2, L2#3; 2024 L2#3).
  - The rebuttal is emotional rather than reasoned, and only one side of a two-sided question is handled (2024 L2#3).
- **Narratives are a list of events in time order**, with a convenient coincidence and a direct moral at the end (2025 L2#1).
- **Loose or unfinished structure.** The opening is unrelated to the body (2024 L2#2), or the essay is unfinished, ending on 「總括而言，」 (2025 L2#2).
- **Sentences run on with commas.** Wording is colloquial or misused, e.g. 「大喜莫及」 (2024 L2#1).
- Often short (about 550–800字), but not always.
- **What separates it from Level 1:** a central idea can still be identified.

### Level 1 (vs Level 2)
- **Meaning breaks down.** The plot jumps without cause or loses track of who is who (2025 L1#1, L1#2; 2024 L1#1). There are digressions (2024 L1#2).
- **The topic is linked by one forced sentence**, or the stimulus is listed with no development (2025 L1#2, L1#3). One vague point is repeated (2024 L1#3).
- **Sentences get in the way of understanding:** 「我以往成任了一位合格的成年人」 (2024 L1#2). Words are misused, e.g. 「快亂的節奏」 (2025 L1#1).
- **Punctuation breaks down**, e.g. whole paragraphs with no full stop (2024 L1#2). Wrong characters are frequent, with heavy crossings-out.
- **Length doesn't save a script.** Scripts of about 950–1,000字 can still be Level 1 (2025 L1#1, 2024 L1#2).

### Progression across levels
- **Argument essays:** counter-examples and a concession, analysed (L5) → two sides set out side by side (L4) → a framework, but contradictory or fence-sitting (L3) → one view, one example (L2) → empty repetition (L1).
- **Narrative and lyrical essays:** a deeper idea, with craft in the imagery and in how the opening and ending echo (L5) → real feeling and detail, but mostly telling (L4) → a complete plot told through stock phrases (L3) → a flat list of events weakly tied to the question (L2) → a jumping plot and broken sentences (L1).

## 5. How the scorer uses this (estimate)

1. Check off-topic and length caps first (§2.3).
2. Count 錯別字 using HKEAA's rules (§2.4), counting a repeated error only once → the 錯別字 mark out of 3.
3. Retrieve **calibration anchors**: exemplar paragraphs at the provisional level and one level either side, the same genre if possible, from the anchor set only.
4. Give a 品第 grade for each of the four criteria. The reason for each must name the §4 markers it observed and cite an anchor (for example, "argument examples are analysed, unlike 2024 L4#3 → 內容 上下").
5. Total out of 103, then map to an estimated level. **Mapping from total to level is an estimate:** HKEAA sets level cut scores after each exam and doesn't publish them. Calibrate this mapping on the held-out exemplars, then on the teacher-marked set.
6. Everything is shown as an estimate, and the teacher's grade overrides it.

## 6. 甲部 (reference only, not scored yet)

These are official facts, kept for when 甲部 is added.

- **Marks:** 50 in total. 內容 30 (marked out of 10, ×3); 行文組織 20 = 行文語氣 10 + 組織 10.
- **Five bands:** 上 9–10, 中上 7–8, 中中 5–6, 中下 3–4, 下 1–2, plus 0. 「先定品，後給分」 [SEM25] slide 26, [BS21] slides 7–8, 28–32.
- **Over the word limit (2025 rule), cap on 內容:** ≤600 → 上 · 601–650 → 中上 · 651–700 → 中中 · 701–750 → 中下 · ≥751 → 下.
- **Format errors** lose marks under 組織: 1 mark for a small slip, one band (2 marks) for bigger or multiple errors. Inventing facts outside the given scenario is not allowed.
- **Needs task-specific marking points for each paper,** which are only published in the paid 評卷參考 (marking guide).
- **Exemplar observations:**
  - 2025 Q1 is a speech. Level 5 covers the format, uses 首先／其次 to set out each activity and its purpose, and ties each point to the audience. Level 1 opens with chit-chat and explains the scheme only in fragments.
  - 2024 Q1 is an open letter.
