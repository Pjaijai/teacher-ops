## 1. Exam structure (verified from the 2029 framework)

| Component | Weighting | Time | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public examination (one paper) | 100% | 2½ h | All questions compulsory. **Section A** 50 marks, 8–12 elementary short questions. **Section B** 50 marks, 3–5 long questions. Total 100 marks. |
| SBA | none | — | No school-based assessment. |

- **Assumed knowledge:** the Compulsory Part plus S1–3 Foundation and Non-foundation Topics.
- Areas: Foundation Knowledge (units 1–5), Calculus (6–11), Algebra (12–17) and a Further Learning Unit (18). Trigonometric formulae are provided in the paper.
- **Calculators and drawing instruments** are allowed. SI and metric units are used.
- **Changes (verified, with a discrepancy):** Section A has been recast from "short questions" to **"elementary short questions"**, aimed at students of average ability. The EDB enhanced-measures page says this applies from the **2027** exam; EDB's M1/M2 slide deck says the Extended Part framework is "applicable to the 2028 HKDSE and onwards". We couldn't reconcile 2027 vs 2028. The content list in this file is unchanged, and a medium-term curriculum content review (possibly splitting Foundation/Non-foundation Topics) is still "under study" only.
- **Syllabus size:** 125 suggested hours. The Extended Part is not subdivided into Foundation and Non-foundation Topics, so there is no FT/NFT marking here (`foundation` is `null` in the JSON).

## 2. Learning Unit tree

**IDs:** `M2-<official unit number>` for a unit (e.g. `M2-3`); objectives are `M2-<unit>.<n>` using the guide's own numbering, so they are stable. Strand names and unit names are the guide's. Suggested lesson hours are the guide's and are for reference only. Machine-readable copy: [m2.json](m2.json).

### Foundation Knowledge

**M2-1 Odd and even functions 奇函數和偶函數** — suggested 2 h
- M2-1.1 Recognise odd and even functions and their graphs; |x| is even.

**M2-2 Mathematical induction 數學歸納法** — suggested 3 h
- M2-2.1 Principle of mathematical induction (first principle); prove propositions on finite sums.
- *Not required / note:* Proving inequalities by induction.

**M2-3 The binomial theorem 二項式定理** — suggested 3 h
- M2-3.1 Expand binomials with positive integer indices; prove the binomial theorem; summation notation.
- *Not required / note:* Trinomials; greatest coefficient/term and coefficient properties; numerical approximation.

**M2-4 More about trigonometric functions 續三角函數** — suggested 15 h
- M2-4.1 Radian measure.
- M2-4.2 Cosecant, secant, cotangent; identities 1+tan²=sec², 1+cot²=cosec²; simplify with identities.
- M2-4.3 Compound angle, double angle, product-to-sum and sum-to-product formulae for sine, cosine, tangent.
- *Not required / note:* 'Subsidiary angle form' (R sin(x+α)).

**M2-5 Introduction to e e 的簡介** — suggested 2 h
- M2-5.1 Definitions and notation of e and the natural logarithm (via lim(1+1/n)^n or the exponential series).
- *Not required / note:* Proving existence of the limit.


### Calculus

**M2-6 Limits 極限** — suggested 3 h
- M2-6.1 Intuitive concept of the limit of a function; limit theorems (no proofs).
- M2-6.2 Find limits, including sinθ/θ→1, (e^x-1)/x→1, and limits of rational functions at infinity.

**M2-7 Differentiation 求導法** — suggested 13 h
- M2-7.1 Concept of the derivative; derive elementary functions (C, x^n, √x, sin, cos, e^x, ln x) from first principles.
- M2-7.2 Sum, product, quotient and chain rules.
- M2-7.3 Derivatives of algebraic, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions.
- M2-7.4 Implicit differentiation (incl. logarithmic differentiation).
- M2-7.5 Second derivative; second derivative test and concavity.
- *Not required / note:* Testing differentiability; third and higher derivatives.

**M2-8 Applications of differentiation 求導法的應用** — suggested 14 h
- M2-8.1 Equations of tangents to a curve.
- M2-8.2 Maximum and minimum values (local and global).
- M2-8.3 Sketch polynomial and rational function curves (symmetry, intercepts, extrema, inflexion, asymptotes incl. oblique via division).
- M2-8.4 Rate of change and optimisation problems.

**M2-9 Indefinite integration and its applications 不定積分法及其應用** — suggested 15 h
- M2-9.1 Concept of indefinite integration.
- M2-9.2 Properties and formulae for algebraic, trigonometric and exponential functions (incl. sin, cos, sec²).
- M2-9.3 Applications in mathematical contexts (e.g. geometry).
- M2-9.4 Integration by substitution.
- M2-9.5 Trigonometric substitutions for integrals involving a²-x², a²+x², x²+a² forms (as listed in the guide); inverse trig notation and principal values.
- M2-9.6 Integration by parts (at most twice per integral).

**M2-10 Definite integration 定積分法** — suggested 10 h
- M2-10.1 Definite integral as a limit of a sum; dummy variables.
- M2-10.2 Properties, including odd/even function symmetry results.
- M2-10.3 Definite integrals of algebraic, trigonometric, exponential functions; Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
- M2-10.4 Definite integration by substitution.
- M2-10.5 Definite integration by parts (at most twice).
- *Not required / note:* Using definite integration to find the sum to infinity of a sequence.

**M2-11 Applications of definite integration 定積分法的應用** — suggested 4 h
- M2-11.1 Area of a plane figure by definite integration.
- M2-11.2 Volume of a solid of revolution about a coordinate axis or a parallel line (disc method).


### Algebra

**M2-12 Determinants 行列式** — suggested 2 h
- M2-12.1 Determinants of order 2 and 3; notation |A|, det A.

**M2-13 Matrices 矩陣** — suggested 10 h
- M2-13.1 Concept, operations (addition, scalar multiple, multiplication) and properties of matrices.
- M2-13.2 Inverses of square matrices of order 2 and 3, and their properties.

**M2-14 Systems of linear equations 線性方程組** — suggested 6 h
- M2-14.1 Solve 2- and 3-variable linear systems by Cramer's rule, inverse matrices, Gaussian elimination; homogeneous systems have nontrivial solutions iff the coefficient matrix is singular.

**M2-15 Introduction to vectors 向量的簡介** — suggested 5 h
- M2-15.1 Vectors vs scalars; magnitude, zero vector, unit vectors; notation.
- M2-15.2 Addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication and their properties.
- M2-15.3 Vectors in the rectangular coordinate system (R² and R³ formulae).
- *Not required / note:* Direction cosines.

**M2-16 Scalar product and vector product 純量積與向量積** — suggested 5 h
- M2-16.1 Definition and properties of the scalar (dot) product.
- M2-16.2 Definition and properties of the vector (cross) product in R³.

**M2-17 Applications of vectors 向量的應用** — suggested 6 h
- M2-17.1 Division of a line segment, parallelism, orthogonality, angle between vectors, projection of a vector, triangle area.


### Further Learning Unit

**M2-18 Inquiry and investigation 探索與研究** — suggested 7 h
- M2-18.1 Discover and construct knowledge through learning activities; build inquiry, communication and reasoning skills.
- *Not required / note:* Not an independent unit; time drawn from other units.
