## 1. Exam structure (verified from the 2029 framework)

| Component | Weighting | Time | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public examination (one paper) | 100% | 2½ h | All questions compulsory. **Section A** 50 marks, 8–12 elementary short questions. **Section B** 50 marks, 3–5 long questions. Total 100 marks. |
| SBA | none | — | No school-based assessment. |

- **Assumed knowledge:** the Compulsory Part plus S1–3 Foundation and Non-foundation Topics.
- Areas: Foundation Knowledge (units 1–2), Calculus (3–9), Statistics (10–20) and a Further Learning Unit (21). Statistical tables are printed in the paper where needed.
- **Calculators and drawing instruments** are allowed. SI and metric units are used.
- **Changes (verified, with a discrepancy):** Section A has been recast from "short questions" to **"elementary short questions"**, aimed at students of average ability. The EDB enhanced-measures page says this applies from the **2027** exam; EDB's M1/M2 slide deck says the Extended Part framework is "applicable to the 2028 HKDSE and onwards". We couldn't reconcile 2027 vs 2028. The content list in this file is unchanged, and a medium-term curriculum content review (possibly splitting Foundation/Non-foundation Topics) is still "under study" only.
- **Syllabus size:** 125 suggested hours. The Extended Part is not subdivided into Foundation and Non-foundation Topics, so there is no FT/NFT marking here (`foundation` is `null` in the JSON).

**IDs:** `M1-<official unit number>` for a unit (e.g. `M1-3`); objectives are `M1-<unit>.<n>` using the guide's own numbering, so they are stable. Strand names and unit names are the guide's. ### Foundation Knowledge

**M1-1 Binomial expansion 二項展式** — suggested 3 h
- M1-1.1 Expand (a+b)^n for positive integer n; use summation notation.
- *Not required / note:* Trinomial expansion; greatest coefficient/term and binomial-coefficient properties; numerical approximation.

**M1-2 Exponential and logarithmic functions 指數函數和對數函數** — suggested 8 h
- M1-2.1 Know the definition of e and the exponential series.
- M1-2.2 Understand y=e^x and y=ln x.
- M1-2.3 Solve problems with exponential/log functions: compound interest, population growth, radioactive decay.
- M1-2.4 Reduce y=k·a^x and y=k·[f(x)]^n to linear relations and read constants from slope/intercepts of a plotted line.


### Calculus

**M1-3 Derivative of a function 函數的導數** — suggested 5 h
- M1-3.1 Intuitive concept of the limit; limit theorems (sum, product, quotient, composite; no proofs).
- M1-3.2 Find limits of algebraic, exponential and logarithmic functions.
- M1-3.3 Concept of the derivative from first principles; notations y', f'(x), dy/dx.
- M1-3.4 Derivative as the slope of the tangent at x=x0.
- *Not required / note:* Finding derivatives from first principles.

**M1-4 Differentiation of a function 函數的求導法** — suggested 8 h
- M1-4.1 Sum, product, quotient and chain rules.
- M1-4.2 Derivatives of algebraic, exponential and logarithmic functions (incl. a^x, log_a x).
- *Not required / note:* Implicit and logarithmic differentiation.

**M1-5 Second derivative 二階導數** — suggested 2 h
- M1-5.1 Concept and notation of the second derivative.
- M1-5.2 Find second derivatives; second derivative test and concavity.
- *Not required / note:* Third and higher derivatives.

**M1-6 Applications of differentiation 求導法的應用** — suggested 10 h
- M1-6.1 Use differentiation for tangents, rates of change, and maximum/minimum problems (local and global extrema).

**M1-7 Indefinite integration and its applications 不定積分法及其應用** — suggested 10 h
- M1-7.1 Concept of indefinite integration as reverse of differentiation.
- M1-7.2 Basic properties and formulae (k, x^n, 1/x, e^x); constant of integration.
- M1-7.3 Integrate algebraic and exponential functions with the basic formulae.
- M1-7.4 Integration by substitution.
- M1-7.5 Use indefinite integration to solve problems.
- *Not required / note:* Integration by parts.

**M1-8 Definite integration and its applications 定積分法及其應用** — suggested 12 h
- M1-8.1 Concept of definite integral as a limit of sums of rectangle areas; dummy variables.
- M1-8.2 Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and properties of definite integrals.
- M1-8.3 Definite integrals of algebraic and exponential functions.
- M1-8.4 Definite integration by substitution.
- M1-8.5 Areas of plane figures by definite integration.
- M1-8.6 Solve problems using definite integration.
- *Not required / note:* Area between a curve and the y-axis, and area between two curves.

**M1-9 Approximation of definite integrals using the trapezoidal rule 運用梯形法則計算定積分的近似值** — suggested 4 h
- M1-9.1 Apply the trapezoidal rule; decide over/under-estimate using the second derivative and concavity.
- *Not required / note:* Error estimation.


### Statistics

**M1-10 Conditional probability and Bayes' theorem 條件概率和貝葉斯定理** — suggested 6 h
- M1-10.1 Concept of conditional probability.
- M1-10.2 Use Bayes' theorem on simple problems.

**M1-11 Discrete random variables 離散隨機變量** — suggested 1 h
- M1-11.1 Concept of discrete random variables.

**M1-12 Probability distribution, expectation and variance 概率分佈、期望值和方差** — suggested 7 h
- M1-12.1 Discrete probability distributions as tables, graphs and formulae.
- M1-12.2 Expectation E(X) and variance Var(X): E(g(X)), E(aX+b), Var(aX+b)=a²Var(X), Var(X)=E(X²)-[E(X)]²; simple problems.

**M1-13 The binomial distribution 二項分佈** — suggested 5 h
- M1-13.1 Concept and properties of the binomial distribution (Bernoulli distribution introduced; mean and variance, no proofs).
- M1-13.2 Compute binomial probabilities.
- *Not required / note:* Use of binomial tables.

**M1-14 The Poisson distribution 泊松分佈** — suggested 5 h
- M1-14.1 Concept and properties of the Poisson distribution (mean and variance, no proofs).
- M1-14.2 Compute Poisson probabilities.
- *Not required / note:* Use of Poisson tables.

**M1-15 Applications of the binomial and the Poisson distributions 二項分佈和泊松分佈的應用** — suggested 5 h
- M1-15.1 Use binomial and Poisson distributions to solve problems.

**M1-16 Basic definition and properties of the normal distribution 正態分佈的基本定義及其性質** — suggested 3 h
- M1-16.1 Continuous random variables and distributions, via the normal distribution; the 12.2 formulae also apply.
- M1-16.2 Properties of the normal distribution (bell-shaped, symmetric, mean=median=mode, spread set by σ, total area 1).
- *Not required / note:* Deriving the mean and variance of the normal distribution.

**M1-17 Standardisation of a normal variable and use of the standard normal table 正態變量的標準化及標準正態分佈表的運用** — suggested 2 h
- M1-17.1 Standardise a normal variable and use the standard normal table.

**M1-18 Applications of the normal distribution 正態分佈的應用** — suggested 7 h
- M1-18.1 Find P(X>x1), P(X<x2), P(x1<X<x2) given x1, x2, μ, σ.
- M1-18.2 Find x given a probability condition.
- M1-18.3 Use the normal distribution to solve problems.

**M1-19 Sampling distribution and point estimates 抽樣分佈和點估計** — suggested 9 h
- M1-19.1 Sample statistics vs population parameters.
- M1-19.2 Sampling distribution of the sample mean: E(X̄)=μ, Var(X̄)=σ²/n; N(μ,σ²/n) when X is normal.
- M1-19.3 Central Limit Theorem.
- M1-19.4 Point estimates: sample mean, sample variance s² (n-1 divisor), unbiased estimator.

**M1-20 Confidence interval for a population mean 總體平均值的置信區間** — suggested 6 h
- M1-20.1 Concept of confidence interval.
- M1-20.2 Find a 100(1-α)% CI for a population mean (known variance with normal population; large n with unknown variance using s).


### Further Learning Unit

**M1-21 Inquiry and investigation 探索與研究** — suggested 7 h
- M1-21.1 Discover and construct knowledge through learning activities; build inquiry, communication and reasoning skills.
- *Not required / note:* Not an independent unit; time drawn from other units.
